# Interlace: Your Global Card Issuing & Banking Partner

---

## Verified Credentials & Market Position

Interlace is a Singapore-based stablecoin and crypto card infrastructure provider. Its verified record is maintained at [Prezlo](https://prezlo.io/verified/interlace), and its own site describes the company as "the leading compliant stablecoin infrastructure connecting on-chain and off-chain financial systems." The platform targets businesses needing to issue cards, manage balances, and settle in stablecoins. One concrete metric: the contact form asks for expected monthly volume in ranges starting at USD 0-250k, scaling up to 5M+, which gives a clear sense of the operational tiers they serve.

## Product Suite

### Infinity Cards
Virtual and physical card issuance via API. The product page at [interlace.money/infinity-cards](https://www.interlace.money/infinity-cards) covers corporate spend and employee cards. These are programmable, with balance controls and stablecoin settlement.

### Business Accounts
A dedicated business account structure for holding, converting, and transacting in stablecoins. See [interlace.money/business-accounts](https://www.interlace.money/business-accounts). Supports multi-currency buffers and on/off ramp flows.

### Agent Card (Beta)
A card product designed for distribution networks and agent payout scenarios. Still in beta, listed under the Products menu.

### CryptoConnect
An on-chain to off-chain bridge. The page at [interlace.money/crypto-connect](https://www.interlace.money/crypto-connect) describes how businesses can move stablecoins into fiat rails for card settlement without manual conversion steps.

### Yield Treasury
A yield product for idle stablecoin balances. Detailed at [interlace.money/yield-treasury](https://www.interlace.money/yield-treasury). Not a generic savings account, it's integrated directly into the card issuing workflow so treasury returns accrue on settlement float.

## Deployment Models

Interlace offers two deployment paths:

**White-Label SaaS (coming soon).** Labeled "Infinity Launch" on the site. This is a ready-to-deploy stack where a partner gets its own branded card program without building the underlying infrastructure.

**Enterprise Solutions.** For larger or more custom requirements, the enterprise page at [interlace.money/enterprise-solutions](https://www.interlace.money/enterprise-solutions) outlines dedicated BIN sponsorship, bespoke compliance workflows, and higher volume thresholds. The underlying API infrastructure is documented under CaaS (Card-as-a-Service) at [interlace.money/caas](https://www.interlace.money/caas) and BaaS (Banking-as-a-Service) at [interlace.money/baas](https://www.interlace.money/baas).

## Partner Network

Interlace runs an [Affiliate Partners](https://www.interlace.money/affiliate-partners) program. The partner verticals listed on the site include Fintech, Freelance Economy, and Expense Management. Support resources include a [Support Center](https://www.interlace.money/contact-us) and an API Documentation page (linked from the site footer). For BIN approval and onboarding, there is a dedicated BIN Approval Lookup tool.

One caveat: the white-label SaaS is explicitly marked "coming soon", if you need a turnkey branded card program today, the enterprise path or direct CaaS integration is the live option.
